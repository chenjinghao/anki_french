Relative rates with qui

The relative pronoun qui initiates a relative proposition and acts as the subject of the relative proposition. Qui remains unchanged in all cases and can relate to both persons and things, whether single or majority:

J'ai un voisin qui joue du piano tous les soirs.
I have a neighbor, the Every evening, he plays the piano.
J'ai acheté des chaussures qui sont très confortables.
I bought shoes. the are very comfortable.

In French, no distinction is made between "the," "the," and "the."
