Relative rates with que

The relative pronoun que initiates a relative set and acts as a direct object of the relative set.

Que is used before vowels and stummem h to qu' and can relate to persons or things, regardless of single or majority:

J'ai un frère que j'admire énormément.
I have a brother, the I admire very much.
J'ai trouvé un restaurant que je recommande à tout le monde.
I found a restaurant, the I recommend everyone.

Again, there is no difference between "the," "the," and "the," in French.
