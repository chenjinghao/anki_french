Inversion with je

The inversion with je is more complicated than with others SubjektpronomenIt is very formal and therefore rare – you should be able to recognize it, but you don't have to use it actively.

Regular verbs on ‑er

For the je‑Form Regular ‑er verb there is a special feature: The mutme ‑e at the end must be pronounced during the inversion. This is indicated by an accent:

je pense → pensé-je / pensè-je
je parle → parlé-je / parlè-je
