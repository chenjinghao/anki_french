Conditional rates with: si

Conditions set out possible, unlikely or not fulfilled conditions. In French, they are often combined with the conjunct si (if) initiated. Depending on the probability, three types of condition sets are distinguished.

I. The Fulfillable Condition

Both condition and consequence are possible and probable here.
