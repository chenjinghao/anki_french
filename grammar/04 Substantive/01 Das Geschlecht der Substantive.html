The sex of the nouns

In French there are only male and female nouns. The German neutrum does not exist. The sex can differ from the German (e.g. la mort – death, le vase – the vase).

Since gender is often only recognized by the article, nouns should always be taught with their article and sex.

The sex of things and things

There are often no logical rules for things, but some groups of words tend to have a certain gender.

Males are usually:

  • Wochentage:
    le lundi (Montag), le vendredi (Freitag)
  • Jahreszeiten:
    le printemps (Spring), l’automne (Herbst)
  • Himmelsrichtungen:
    le sud (South), le nord (Norden)
  • Sprachen:
    le portugais (Portuguese), l’italien (Italian)
  • Trees:
    le chêne (Eiche), le sapin (Tanne)
  • Metals & chemical elements:
    l’or (Gold), le mercure (Quecksilber)
  • Transportmittel:
    le bus (Bus), le train (Zug), l’avion (Flugzeug)
  • Countries that don't end up on ‑e:
    le Canada (Kanada), le Brésil (Brasilien)

Females are usually:

  • Countries that end up on ‑e:
    la France (Frankreich), la Suisse (Schweiz)
    6 Ausnahmen: le Belize (Belize), le Cambodge (Kambodscha), le Mexique (Mexiko), le Mozambique (Mosambik), le Suriname, le Zimbabwe (Simbabwe)
  • Kontinente:
    l’Europe (Europa), l’Asie (Asien)
  • Rivers:
    la Saône, la Moselle
    Ausnahmen: le Rhône, le Danube
  • Science:
    la géographie (Geographie), la médecine (Medizin)
    Ausnahme: le droit (Law)
  • Autonamen:
    la BMW, la Citroën
