The plural of the nouns

Most nouns have a plural in French. certain Articles les or with the indeterminate Article des gebildet.

Regular plural on ‑s

Most nouns receive the ending in plural ‑s. This ‑s is not pronounced.

Singular Plural
le livre
the book
les livres
the books
la table
the table
les tables
Tables

Plural on ‑ s, ‑ x, ‑ z

Nouns who are in the singular on ‑s, ‑x or ‑z end, remain unchanged in the plural.

Singular Plural
le prix
the price
les prix
the prices
le nez
the nose
les nez
the noses
le bus
the bus
les bus
the buses

Plural at special endings

Nouns on ‑au, ‑eu and ‑ou received in the plural a ‑x.
Nouns on ‑al and some on ‑ail get the ending ‑aux.
