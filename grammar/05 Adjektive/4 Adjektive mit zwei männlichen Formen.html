Adjectives with two male forms

The three adjectives beau (Beautiful), nouveau (neu) and vieux (alt) have two forms in the male singular. The form depends on whether the following noun with a consonant or with a vowel or a vowel stummen h beginnt.

beau (Beautiful)

masculine feminine
Singular
un beau parc
a Beautiful Park
un bel arbre
a Beautiful Baum
une belle fleur
one beautiful Blume
une belle histoire
one beautiful Geschichte
Plural
de(s) beaux parcs
beautiful Parks
de(s) beaux arbres
beautiful Trees
de(s) belles fleurs
beautiful Blumen
de(s) belles histoires
beautiful Geschichten

Other cases

Also the adjectives fou (Crazy) and mou (weich) form a second male singular form (fol, mol) Since these adjectives are usually used to be replaced, the forms occur only in fixed turns:

un fol espoir
one in vain Hoffnung
un mol abandon
a schwaches Nachgeben
