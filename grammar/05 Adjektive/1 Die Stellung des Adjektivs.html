The position of the adjective

Unlike in German, most adjectives are in French hinter the noun they describe.

The majority of adjectives, in particular multi-syllable adjectives and those which designate colours, shapes or nationalities, are re-enacted:

un film intéressant
an interesting film
une personne sympathique
a sympathetic person
une voiture blanche
a white car
une histoire vraie
a true story
la musique française
the French music

However, a small group of short and frequently used adjectives are used before the noun. The acronym serves as a memorabilia GABS:

G – Size (e.g. grand, petit, gros)

une grande maison
a large house

A – Alter (z.B. jeune, vieux, nouveau)

un vieil ami
an old friend

B – evaluation/goods (e.g. bon, mauvais)

une bonne idée
a good idea

S – Beauty (e.g. beau, joli)

un bel appartement
a nice apartment

Some adjectives change their meaning, depending on whether they are in front of or behind the noun.

  • Is the adjective before the noun, the meaning is often subjective or figurative.
  • Is the adjective hinter the noun, the meaning is more objective or literal.
