The increase in adjectives

In order to form the comparative, one sets plus (superiority), moins (inferiority) or aussi (Equality) before the adjective. The comparison word is then used with que angeschlossen.

Lyon est plus grand que Nice.
Lyon is Larger as Nice.
Lyon est moins grand que Paris.
Lyon is kleiner as Paris.
Lyon est aussi grand que Marseille.
Lyon is just as big like Marseille.

The French comparative of inferiority with moins is usually expressed in German by the comparative of the opposite adjective, for example:

Sophie est grande, mais elle est moins grande que sa mère.
Sophie's big, but she's big. kleiner as her mother.

The superlative is formed by: le/la/les plus (superiority) or le/la/les moins (Inferiority) before the adjective. The adjective is adjusted in number and sex to the noun to which it refers.

Quel est le lac le plus profond du monde?
Which is the tiefste Lake of the world?

The connection to the reference word takes place by means of the preposition de. For short, commonly used adjectives, the superlative can also stand before the noun:

Antoine est le plus jeune membre de l'équipe.
Antoine is that Recent member of the team.

The Adjectives bon and mauvais have irregular forms of increase: meilleur (better) and pire (schlimmer).

Cette solution est meilleure que l'autre.
This solution is: besser than the other.
C'est le meilleur film de l'année.
This is the beste film of the year.
La situation est pire que prévu.
The situation is schlimmer than expected.
C'est le pire moment pour partir.
This is the schlechteste Time to go.
