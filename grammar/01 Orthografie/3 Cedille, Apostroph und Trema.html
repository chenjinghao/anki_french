Cedille, apostrophe and trema

In addition to the accents, there are the Cedille (ç), the Apostroph (-) and the Trema (¨).

The Cedille (ç)

The cedille below the c (ç) indicates that it is before the vowels a, o and u like a voiceless s \s\ or

Ça va bien, merci.
Es geht gut, danke.
un garçon
A boy
J'ai reçu un message.
I got a message.

The Apostrophe (-)

The apostrophe indicates that a vowel falls away at the end of the word (elision) when the following word is used with a vowel or a vowel. stummen h beginnt.

l'ordinateur (le + ordinateur)
the computer
l'heure (la + heure)
the hour

The Trema ( ̈)

The treme ( ̈) above a vowel indicates that this vowel is pronounced separately from the previous vowel.

naïf
naiv
Joyeux Noël !
Frohe Weihnachten!
