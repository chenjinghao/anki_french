Accents on capital letters

In the past, accents on capital letters were often omitted, as this was technically difficult on typewriters. Even today, in advertising or in handwritten notes, capital letters are still often seen without accents.

According to the Académie française However, the accent is an integral part of the spelling. It should also be set to capital letters, since its absence disturbs the reading flow or can completely change the meaning of a sentence.

UN INTERNE TUE
An intern kills / A killed intern kills / An inmate kills / A killed inmate

Without accents four different interpretations are possible! With correct accenting the meaning becomes clear.

UN INTERNÉ TUÉ
A killed inmate
