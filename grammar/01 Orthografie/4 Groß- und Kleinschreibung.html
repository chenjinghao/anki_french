Case-sensitive

In French, it is generally downgraded. Capitalization is used less often than in German. Above all, nouns that are not proper names are downgraded.

la maison
the house

The following shall be capitalised:

  • Starting of the sentence:
    Le président parle à la télé.
    The president's on TV.
  • Names and surnames:
    Marie-Antoinette, Jean Gabin
  • Names of inhabitants (countries, regions):
    les Anglais, une Suédoise
    the English, a Swede

    Adjectives of nationality are downgraded:
    le fromage suisse

  • Geographical names (countries, rivers, mountains):
    la Manche, les Alpes
  • directions, if they are meant as a geographical region:
    le Sud de la France
    the South of France
  • Names of festivals and holidays:
    Pâques, Noël
    Ostern, Weihnachten
  • Names of institutions and companies:
    la Sorbonne, Renault
  • Religious Names and Sacred Scriptures:
    la Bible, le Coran, Dieu
  • Anredeformen:
    Madame, Monsieur, Mademoiselle
