The Alphabet

The French alphabet has 26 letters. Their names and pronunciation differ in part from the German.

a \a\ j \ʒi\ s \ɛs\
b \be\ k \ka\ t \te\
c \se\ l \ɛl\ u \y\
d \de\ m \ɛm\ v \ve\
e \ə\ n \ɛn\ w \dubləve\
f \ɛf\ o \o\ x \iks\
g \ʒe\ p \pe\ y \igrɛk\
h \aʃ\ q \ky\ z \zɛd\
i \i\ r \ɛr\

In addition, there are orthographic signs: the three Akzente (accent aigu ´, accent grave `, accent circonflexe ^), the Cedille (ç) and the Trema (¨).

The letters k and w are mainly used in foreign words.

le kilo
the kilo
le week-end
the weekend
