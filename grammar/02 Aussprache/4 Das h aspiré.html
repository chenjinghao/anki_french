The h aspiré

In French, the letter h At the beginning of the word always mute. However, one distinguishes two types, which behave differently grammatically: the h muet (stummes h) and the h aspiré (behauchtes h).

The term "sprayed h" is historically conditioned. h aspiré The only difference is whether the liaison and an apostrophe (elision) with the previous word is possible or not.

The h muet (stummes h)

The h muet acts like a vowel. It allows the elimination (merging with apostrophe) and the Liaison.

l'histoire
History
les‿histoires
the stories (pronounced: \lɛ‿zistwar\)

The h aspiré (sprayed h)

The h aspiré acts like a consonant. It prevents the elimination and the liaison. A small pause between the words arises.

le hasard
chance (not l’hasard)
les hasards
Incidents (pronounced: \le azar\, not \le‿zazar\)
