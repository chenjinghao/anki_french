Demonstrative pronouns (celui)

With the demonstrative pronouns you select something specific from a group.

Person einfach zusammengesetzt
maskulin Singular celui celui-ci / celui-là
Plural ceux ceux-ci / ceux-là
feminin Singular celle celle-ci / celle-là
Plural celles celles-ci / celles-là
Je parle de celui au centre.
I'm talking about The in the middle.

This form is used in the following cases:

  • the demonstrative pronoun is used with de added:
    Les livres sur la table sont ceux de l'étudiant.
    The books on the table belong to the student.
  • The demonstrative pronoun follows a Relativsatz:
    Les livres sur la table sont ceux que l'étudiant a laissés.
    The books on the table are theThat the student left behind.

In addition to the simple forms of the demonstrative pronoun, there are the composite forms with ‑ci and ‑là. Demonstrative pronouns with ‑ci point to something that is in the immediate vicinity of the speaker (here). ‑là refer to something further away (there).

Lequel ? Celui-ci ou celui-là ?
Welches? This one or that there?
