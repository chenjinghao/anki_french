The Indefinite Companions

Indefinite companions stand with a noun. They denote an indefinite quantity or an indefinite identity.

aucun(e), pas un(e), nul(le): None, not a single one

These terms shall be used in: negative sentences used to express the absence of a certain quantity:

Bravo! Vous n'avez fait aucune faute!
You've got it. keinen Fehler gemacht!
Elle n'avait nulle envie de le revoir.
She had none Lust, ihn wiederzusehen.
Pas une seule fenêtre n'est ouverte.
None (only) window is open.
nul is only used in the upscale language.

autre(s): anderer, andere

Used to express an alternative or a difference:

Je prendrais bien une autre part de tarte.
I'd like to have another Pieces Torte nehmen.
Before autre always stands either an article or a demonstrator, possessive or indefinite companion. It changes in number.

certain(e)s: manche, gewisse

Used to express an indefinite amount or selection:

Certains spectateurs ont sifflé.
Manche Spectators have whistled.
Tu ne trouves pas qu'il a un certain charme?
Don't you think he's a gewissen Has charm?

chaque: jede(r,s)

Refers to each individual element of a group:

Chaque élève a reçu un livre.
Jeder Pupils have received a book.
chaque is immutable in sex and number.

divers(es), différent(e)s: various

Used to express diversity or diversity:

Divers facteurs influencent l'économie mondiale.
Various Factors affect the global economy.
divers and différents They are always plural and adapt to the noun in the sex.

le/la/les même(s): the same

Used to point out something already mentioned:

C'est lui qui a préparé le repas, même le dessert.
He prepared the food, sogar The dessert.
même adapts to gender and number. Without article is même an adverb (even) and unchangeable.
