The sequence of times in indirect speech

The time form in the auxiliary sentence depends on the time form of the initiating verb in the main sentence.

Introductory verb in the present or future

Is the introductory verb in the present (Présent) or in the future (Futur), the time form of the auxiliary sentence does not change.
