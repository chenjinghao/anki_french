Limitations by: ne ... que and seulement

Sowohl ne ... que and seulement can be used to express a limitation.

Les Dupont n’ont qu’un enfant.
Les Dupont ont seulement un enfant.
The Duponts have only A child.

In the spoken language prevails (ne) ... que, also because the ne can be omitted (Informelle Verneinung).
