Negation words

In French, the negation usually consists of two parts: ne (or n’ before vowel, or stummem h) and a second negation word. This differs from the German, where the negation usually consists of only one word.

ne ... pas not
ne ... plus not more
ne ... jamais nie
ne ... plus jamais never again
ne ... rien nichts
ne ... plus rien nothing more
ne ... personne niemand
ne ... plus personne No more
ne ... guère kaum
ne ... pas encore Not yet
ne ... toujours pas still not
ne ... pas toujours not always
ne ... ni ... ni neither ... nor
ne ... pas ... ni neither ... nor
ne ... pas ... du tout Not at all
ne ... nulle part nirgends
ne ... point Not at all

Examples of application:

Marie n’aime pas le café.
Marie mag keinen Kaffee.
Nous ne voyons plus nos voisins.
We see our neighbors not more.
Ils ne vont jamais au cinéma.
You're leaving nie to the movies.
Je ne connais personne ici.
I know here niemanden.

In colloquial language, the ne often omitted, but in the written language it should always be used:

Je (ne) sais pas.
I know not.
