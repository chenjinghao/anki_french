Questions with qui

If you have any questions with qui the subject, one can ease life, since the question with qui always without est-ce qui can be formed, e.g.:

Qui habite à Lyon ?
Who lives in Lyon?

Instead of qui you can also qui est-ce qui use if you want to ask about the subject.

Qui est-ce qui habite à Lyon ?
Who lives in Lyon?

If: qui connected to a preposition, then you die est-ce que Not around.

The question about the direct object is: qui est-ce que:

Qui est-ce que vous cherchez ?
Who are you looking for?

The question about the indirect object is: à qui est-ce que:

À qui est-ce que tu penses ?
Who are you thinking about?
