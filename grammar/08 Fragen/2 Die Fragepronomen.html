The question pronouns

In French there are various question pronouns:

à quelle heure What time?
à qui who, who
à quoi what for
combien how much
combien de temps how long
comment how
d’où woher
de qui by whom
de quoi wovon
depuis quand since when
où where, where
pourquoi warum
quand wann
que was
quel(s), quelle(s) welcher, welche, welches
qui who

It is particularly important to ask the following questions: est-ce quebecause it is always common and correct.

Quand est-ce que tu ranges ta chambre?
When are you cleaning up your room?
Où est-ce que tu as trouvé ton sac?
Where did you find your bag?
Pourquoi est-ce que vous étudiez le français?
Why are you studying French?
Qu’est-ce qu’il fait demain?
What's he doing tomorrow?

In contrast to German, the question pronoun can also be re-examined in French:

Tu habites où ?
Where do you live?

The formation of the question with the question pronoun is simple, since the statement is simply followed by the corresponding question pronoun. The position of the sentence members in the statement pronoun is preserved:

Tu t’appelles comment?
What's your name?
Tu arrives d’où?
Woher kommst du?

Quoi will be at the end of the sentence instead of que is used.

Que fais-tu ?
Qu'est-ce que tu fais ?
Tu fais quoi?
Was machst du?
