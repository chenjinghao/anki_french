Bruchzahlen

Most fractions are derived from the order numbers. The denominator (the lower number) corresponds to the Ordnungszahl.

The exceptions are the first three breaks: un demi (a half), un tiers (a third) and un quart (a quarter).

½ un demi
⅓ un tiers
¼ un quart
⅕ un cinquième
⅙ un sixième
⅘ quatre cinquièmes
⅚ cinq sixièmes
8⁄10 huit dixièmes

If the counter (the upper number) is greater than 1, the order number in the denominator is given a plural‑s.
