The order numbers are formed by the ending ‑ième to the respective basic number.

  • For basic figures, which are based on: ‑e This is the case in the United Kingdom. ‑e weg: quatre → quatrième.
  • Ausnahmen: un → premier/première and cinq → cinquième, neuf → neuvième.
  • Da premier can not be connected to other numbers, one uses from 21 unième: vingt et unième.

The article before the order numbers is never apostrophed, even before vowel or stummem h: la huitième, le onzième.

Dates and rulers

In French, only for the ersten day of one month and for the ersten Rulers of a name use the order number. For all following days or rulers the basic number is used.

le premier mai
the first May
François premier
Franz the First
le deux juin, le trois juillet
the second of June, the third of July
Louis quatorze
Louis the Fourteenth

Expressions with ‘everyone/everyone/everyone'

An expression such as "every other day" is used in French with un/une + Grundzahl + sur + basic number or tous les/toutes les + Grundzahl wiedergegeben.

Elle rend visite à son père un jour sur deux.
She visits her father jeden zweiten Tag.
Elle rend visite à son père tous les deux jours.
She visits her father every two days.
