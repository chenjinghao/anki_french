Verb + Infinitiv

The infinitive can stand without preposition after a conjugated verb. This is especially true of the Modalverben, the verbs faire and laisser and in the case of verbs of movement or perception.

After the modal adjuvants

The modal verbs form the largest group of verbs that produce a pure infinitive.

pouvoir (#20) may:
Peux‑tu m'aider, s'il te plaît ?
Kannst du mir bitte helfen?
devoir (#39) the
Je dois finir ce rapport avant midi.
I'm sorry. must: complete this report before noon.
vouloir (#57) wollen
Les enfants veulent aller au parc.
The children wollen go to the park.
sembler (#180) scheinen
Pierre semble comprendre la situation.
Pierre scheint to understand the situation.
avoir beau (#393) try in vain
Elle a beau essayer, elle n'y arrive pas.
They versucht es in vainShe's not gonna make it.
paraître (#519) scheinen
Il paraît savoir la réponse.
Er scheint the answer.
oser (#1634) wagen
Il n'ose pas lui parler.
Er wagt I don't want to talk to her.
faillir (#3505) beinahe tun
J'ai failli rater mon train.
I'm sorry. had beinahe meinen Zug verpasst.

After faire and laisser

The verbs faire (make, arrange) and laisser (let) are often used with an infinitive to express that the subject does something not itself, but rather lets it do it.

faire + Infinitiv etwas tun lassen
Je fais réparer ma voiture.
I'm sorry. lasse my car reparieren.
laisser + Infinitiv (to) allow something to be done
Il a laissé tomber ses clés.
He's got his keys fallen lassen.
