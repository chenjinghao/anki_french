Participe passé

The participe passé is the verb form which is used for the formation of compound forms of time (as the passé composé) and the Passivs It is variable in sex and number.

The participe passé is formed by hanging the following endings to the verb trunk:

Verbgruppe Endung Example
Verbs on -er -é
manger → mangé
gegessen
Verbs on -ir -i
finir → fini
beendet
Verbs on -re -u
attendre → attendu
gewartet

Many verbs form the participe passé irregular. The most important are:

avoir eu gehabt
être été gewesen
faire fait gemacht
prendre pris genommen
mettre mis gestellt
voir vu gesehen

Angleichung

The rules for alignment depend on the auxiliary verb (être or avoir) ab.

Commission être

The participe passé will immer aligned to the subject of the sentence in gender and number. This applies to:

  • Certain verbs of movement in the passé composé
  • All reflexiven Verben

Commission avoir

The participe passé with avoir is Invariable, except there is a direktes Objekt (accumulator), the before the verb Then the participle is aligned to this object.

The pre-existing direct object can be:

  • A direktes Objektpronomen (me, te, le, la, nous, vous, les)
    • Tu aimes ces chansons ? Je les ai écoutées hier.
    Do you like these songs? Member of the.

  • A Fragewort (quel, combien de, ...)
    • Combien de livres as-tu lus cet été ?
