Gérondif

The gérondif (Gerundium) is a common form that describes an action that takes place simultaneously with the action of the main proposition. The subject of both actions is always the same.

The gérondif is always Invariable. One forms it with the preposition en + participe présent.

Sophie a découvert un nouveau restaurant en se promenant dans le quartier.
Sophie discovered a new restaurant, while they through the quarter spazierte.
