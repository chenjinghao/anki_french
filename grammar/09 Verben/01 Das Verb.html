The verb

The verb is the central element of a sentence and describes:

  • one action, z.B. manger (essen), parler (sprechen)
  • one Zustand, z.B. être (sein), sembler (scheinen)
  • or a Ereignis, z.B. pleuvoir (regnen), arriver (ankommen)

The Infinitive end up in French ‑er, ‑ir, ‑re or ‑oir. These endings determine the affiliation to a conjugation group and thus the respective endings in the different time forms.

The French verbs are divided into three main conjugation groups. ‑oir form a separate group of irregular verbs.

Verbs to ‑er:

manger (essen) → Thomas mange une pomme.
Thomas isst an apple.
jouer (spielen) → Nous jouons aux échecs.
We spielen Schach.

Verbs to ‑

finir (beenden) → Ils finissent leur repas.
They beenden ihre Mahlzeit.
dormir (schlafen) → Le bébé dort bien.
The baby Sleeps gut.

Verbs on ‑re and Verbs to ‑oir:

perdre (verlieren) → J'ai perdu mon chapeau.
I've got my hat verloren.
recevoir (bekommen) → Il reçoit une décoration.
Er bekommt an award.

Within these groups there is a distinction between regular and irregular verbs. In addition, verbs can be divided according to their function in Vollverben, Hilfsverben, Modalverben, reflexive Verben and Impersonal verbs.
