The verbs on ‑er

The verbs on ‑er form the largest group (about 90% of all verbs) and are all (except alleror

The endings in the Present lauten ‑e, ‑es, ‑e, ‑ons, ‑ez, ‑ent. Example of verb donner (geben):

je je me donne
tu tu te donnes
il/elle/on il/elle/on se donne
nous nous nous donnons
vous vous vous donnez
ils/elles ils/elles se donnent

The participe passé to: ‑é gebildet:

parler (sprechen) → j'ai parlé
I have been gesprochen

Special features of spelling:

  • In the case of verbs on ‑cer the c before o and a to a ç, to the \s\‑Aussprache beizubehalten.
commencer (anfangen) → nous commençons
we're starting
  • In the case of verbs on ‑ger is a e between g and o/a added to the \ʒ\‑ to be discussed.
manger (essen) → nous mangeons
we eat
  • In the case of verbs on ‑yer the y before a mute e to a i.
employer (verwenden) → j'emploie
I am using
  • In the case of verbs on ‑eler and ‑eter The consonant is doubled before the end.
appeler (rufen) → j'appelle, but: nous appelons
I'm calling, but we're calling
jeter (werfen) → je jette, but: nous jetons
I throw, but we throw
  • For verbs with ‑e- or ‑é- in the trunk the accent changes to ‑è-.
se lever (aufstehen) → je me lève, but: nous nous levons
I get up, but we get up
répéter (wiederholen) → je répète, but: vous répétez
I repeat, but: you repeat
