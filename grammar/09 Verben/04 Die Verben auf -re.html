The verbs on ‑re

There are about 180 verbs on ‑reMany of these are irregular, but there is a small group of regular verbs, e.g. vendre (verkaufen).

The regular endings in the Present lauten: ‑s, ‑s, -∅, ‑ons, ‑ez, ‑ent. The participe passé Ends ‑u.

je je me vends
tu tu te vends
il/elle/on il/elle/on se vend
nous nous nous vendons
vous vous vous vendez
ils/elles ils/elles se vendent
Il attend le bus.
Er wartet on the bus.
