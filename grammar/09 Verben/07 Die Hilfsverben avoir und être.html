The Auxiliary Verbs avoir and être

The verbs avoir (have) and être (sein) are the most important verbs in French. They serve as full verbs as well as auxiliary verbs for the formation of the composite times.

avoir as a full verb: Sophie a une voiture rouge.
Sophie has a red car.
avoir as an auxiliary verb: J'ai perdu la clé.
I'm sorry. habe the key verloren.

Use of: avoir as an auxiliary

Avoir is used to form composite times at most verbs:

  • Most of the intransitiven Verben:

Use of: être as an auxiliary

Être is used for the formation of composite times in the following cases:

  • for all Passivformen:
    • Les piscines seront rouvertes le mois prochain.
    The swimming pools will be next month reopened.
  • for all reflexiven Verben:
    • Ils étaient fâchés, mais ils se sont expliqués.
    They were divided, but they have spoken out.
