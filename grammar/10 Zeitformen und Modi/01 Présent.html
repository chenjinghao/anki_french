Présent

The présent (Presence) is a simple form of time of the indicative. It describes, similar to in German, present events and actions that take place at the time of speaking, as well as habits and general facts.

je j’ je me je m’
tu tu te tu t’
il il se il s’
nous nous nous
vous vous vous
ils ils se ils s’

The present describes an event that takes place at the time of communication.

Sophie et Lucas préparent le dîner.
Sophie and Lucas are preparing dinner.

With depuis (since) the present can express an action that has begun in the past and is still ongoing.

Emma étudie le français depuis deux ans.
Emma's been studying French for two years.

It can also describe an event in the near future.

Nous commençons notre nouveau projet la semaine prochaine.
We'll start our new project next week.

The present is used for habits, general knowledge and proverbs.

Les Martin font du sport tous les weekends.
The Martins do sports every weekend.
La patience est une vertu.
Patience is a virtue.

After si (if) the present expresses a realistic condition or hypothesis.

Si nous prenons le train de 8h, nous arriverons à temps pour la réunion.
If we take the train at 8 o'clock, we'll arrive in time for the meeting.

In narratives, the present can be used to depict past events more vividly (historical present).

Je regardais la télévision. Soudain, on sonne à la porte.
I was watching TV, and suddenly it rings at the door.
