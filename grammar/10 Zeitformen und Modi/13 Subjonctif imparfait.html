The subjonctif imparfait is a literary form of time that occurs exclusively in the upscale written language. In the spoken language and modern texts, instead, the subjonctif présent is used.

It is used when the verb in the main sentence is in a past form (e.g. imparfait, passé simple) and the act in the secondary sentence is simultaneously or subsequently.
