Passé simple

The passé simple is a simple past form, which is mainly used in the written language (e.g. in novels or history books). In German it is usually translated with the preteritum (imperfect) or the perfect.

je j’ je me je m’
tu tu te tu t’
il il se il s’
nous nous nous
vous vous vous
ils ils se ils s’

The passé simple has similar functions to the passé composébut has no relation to the present. In the spoken language, the passé composé often instead of the passé simple is used.

The passé simple is used to express completed events, successive actions or limited repetitions in the past.

Guy de Maupassant mourut en 1893.
Guy de Maupassant starb 1893.
Sophie ouvrit la porte, puis entra dans la pièce.
Sophie opened the door and then entered the room.
Pendant un mois, elle lui écrivit chaque jour.
For a month she wrote to him every day.

In written texts are often passé simple and imparfait or passé composé and imparfait The passé simple or passé composé describes the main action, while the imparfait describes the background or state.

Le train arriva à la gare qui était bondée de voyageurs.
The train arrived at the train station, which was full of travellers.
