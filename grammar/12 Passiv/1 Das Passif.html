The Passif

Im passif (Passive) is the subject not of the person acting, but of the recipient of the action. Passiveness is used in French less frequently than in German and occurs mainly in the written language.

The passive consists of the Hilfsverb être (= become) + participe passé. Être stands in the same time as the verb in the active sentence.

Lucas a préparé un gâteau délicieux.
(Aktiv)
Lucas baked a delicious cake.
Un gâteau délicieux a été préparé par Lucas.
(Passiv)
A delicious cake was by Lucas gebacken.

The person acting is usually prepositioned par In the case of the United Kingdom.

Cette chanson a été composée par un musicien talentueux.
This song was composed by a talented musician.

For some verbs that describe a state or are used in the figurative sense, the preposition de bevorzugt:

Ce professeur est respecté de tous ses élèves.
This teacher will of all his students respektiert.
La ville est entourée de montagnes magnifiques.
The City is of beautiful mountains umgeben.
