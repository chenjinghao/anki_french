The position of adverbs

1. Adverbs of the place and the specified time

Adverbia, which is a place (ici, là-bas) or a certain period (demain, hier), are flexible at the beginning of the sentence or at the end of the sentence.

Demain, je vais à la piscine.
Morning I'm going to the swimming pool.
Je vais à la piscine demain.
I'm going Tomorrow into the swimming pool.

The Adverbs tôt, tard and ensemble are an exception. In composite times they always stand hinter participe passé and infinitive constructions hinter The Infinitiv.

3. Adverbs commenting on the whole sentence

Adverbs referring to the whole sentence (e.g. heureusement, malheureusement), are usually at the beginning or end of the sentence and are separated by a comma.

Heureusement, je n'ai pas oublié mon parapluie.
Luckily, I haven't forgotten my umbrella.
