The increase in adverbs

The adverb can be same as the adjective steigern.

Elle court vite.
She's running schnell.

The comparative of the adverb is plus ... que or moins ... que the comparison with aussi ... que.

Elle court plus vite que son mari.
She's running schneller as her husband.
Elle court moins vite que son mari.
She's running langsamer as her husband.
Elle court aussi vite que son mari.
She's running genauso schnell Like her husband.

The superlative is used with le plus ... (de) and le moins ... (de) gebildet.

Elle court le plus vite de tous.
She runs from all of them on schnellsten.
Elle court le moins vite de tous.
She runs from all of them on langsamsten.

There are also irregular forms of increase:

bien – mieux – le mieux
gut – besser – am besten
beaucoup – plus – le plus
much – more – most
peu – moins – le moins
little – less – least

The Form pis is an older comparative form of mal and is used today mainly in fixed turns:

Tant pis ! On reviendra demain.
Macht nichts! / Pech gehabt! We'll be back tomorrow.
La situation va de mal en pis.
The situation will: immer schlechter.
Les choses vont de pis en pis.
Things are going to immer schlimmer.

In modern language, for the comparative of mal meist plus mal or moins bien is used.
