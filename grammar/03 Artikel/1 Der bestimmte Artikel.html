The specific Article (le, la)

The specific article (article défini) is based on the Gender and the number of the noun.

masculine feminine
Singular
le train
the train
l'hôtel
the hotel
la question
the question
l'école
School
Plural
les trains
trains
les hôtels
the hotels
les questions
the questions
les écoles
schools

Before nouns, with a vowel or stummem h start, the articles will le and la to l' This process is called elimination.

The s at the end of les is normally mute. However, if a word starts with a vowel or mute h, it will be like a vocal s \z\ and associated with the following word. Liaison (Bindung).

les‿amis
the Friends
les‿oranges
the oranges

The specific Articles le and les merge with the prepositions à and de on new forms:

à + le = au → On va au restaurant.
go to the restaurant
à + les = aux → une tarte aux fraises
a strawberry cake
de + le = du → le livre du garçon
the book of the boy
de + les = des → la voiture des parents
the car of the parents

Usage

In French, the particular article is used in some cases where it does not appear in German:

  • in the case of generalisations:
    J’adore la musique classique.
    I love classical music.
  • in the case of titles:
    Le professeur Martin n'est pas là aujourd'hui.
    Professor Martin isn't here today.
  • in the case of parts of the body:
    Elle a les mains froides.
    She's got cold hands.
  • for languages and fixed phrases:
    Elle parle le japonais.
    She speaks Japanese.

The particular article is used in French for most country names.

Je visite l'Allemagne.
I'm visiting Germany.
Il vient du Portugal.
He's from Portugal.
Nous allons aux États-Unis.
We're going to the USA.
La Normandie est une belle région.
Normandy is a beautiful region.

In the case of female country names, the article is not used if it is prepositioned en or de / d' stehen:

Mon ami voyage en France, mais il vient d'Italie.
My friend travels to France, but he's from Italy.
