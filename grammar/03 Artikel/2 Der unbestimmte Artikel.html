The Indeterminate Article (un, une)

The Indeterminate Article (article indéfini) is based on the Gender and the number of the noun and designates an indefinite person or thing.

masculine feminine
Singular
un livre
a book
une table
a table
Plural
des livres / des tables
Books / Tables

In German there is no indefinite article in the plural. In French, for this there is no indeterminate article in the plural. des It corresponds to the German plural without article or also "some".

J'ai un chien.
I've been one Hund.
J'ai des chiens.
I have dogs.

Sonderfall: de After a denial

After a Verneinung (z.B. ne ... pas, ne ... plus), the indeterminate Articles un, une and des to de (or d’ before vowel/mute h).

Paul a un frère. → Paul n'a pas de frère.
Paul has one Brother. Paul doesn't have a brother.
J'ai une voiture. → Je n'ai plus de voiture.
I've been a Car. I don't have a car anymore.
Elle a des amis. → Elle n'a pas d’amis.
She has friends. She has no friends.

Sonderfall: de before an adjective in the plural

If the noun is preceded by an adjective in the plural, the article des to de (or d’).

J'ai acheté des fleurs.
I bought flowers.
J'ai acheté de belles fleurs.
I bought beautiful flowers.
Nous avons visité des monuments intéressants.
We have visited interesting monuments.
Nous avons visité d’anciens monuments.
We have visited old monuments.
