Rule in the case of phrases

In many fixed phrases and expressions, no article is used in French, deviating from the basic rules.

Verbs used (excluding articles)

Especially for common verbs such as avoir and faire there are many fixed phrases without articles.

avoir faim / soif Hunger / thirsty
Après la randonnée, nous avions très faim.
After the migration we were very hungry.
avoir peur de Scared of
Le petit garçon a peur du noir.
The little boy is afraid of The darkness.
faire attention aufpassen
Fais attention en traversant la rue !
Watch outWhen you cross the road!
rendre service à Doing someone a favor
Peux-tu me rendre un service ?
Kannst du mir Doing a favor?

Prepositional twists (excluding articles)

Also many expressions, which are used with a Preposition start, come out without articles.

en voiture / train / avion by car / train / plane
Nous voyageons en train pour des raisons écologiques.
We travel for ecological reasons by train.
à pied / vélo on foot / by bike
Je vais au travail à pied.
I'm going On foot to work.
par cœur auswendig
Il faut apprendre ce poème par cœur.
You have to write this poem auswendig lernen.

Proverbs (without articles)

In proverbs, articles are often omitted to make a general statement.

Noblesse oblige.
Adel verpflichtet.
Pierre qui roule n'amasse pas mousse.
A rolling stone doesn't put moss on.
