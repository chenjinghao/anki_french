Beiordnende Konjunktionen

Conjunctions attach two sentences or parts of sentences which are grammatically equivalent, e.g. two main sentences.

Le soleil brille et les oiseaux chantent.
The sun is shining and The birds sing.
Je voulais acheter ce livre, mais je n'avais pas assez d'argent.
I wanted to buy this book, but I didn't have enough money.

The conjuncts which accompany the application include:

car denn
Il reste à la maison, car il est fatigué.
He's staying at home, denn He's tired.
donc So, therefore,
Il pleut, donc je prends mon parapluie.
Es regnet, also I'll take my umbrella.
et and
J'aime le chocolat et le café.
I like chocolate and Kaffee.
mais but, but
Le film est long, mais très intéressant.
The movie is long, but Very interesting.
ni ... ni neither ... nor
Il ne mange ni viande ni poisson.
Er isst neither Meat nor Fish.
or well, but
Je voulais acheter du pain ; or la boulangerie était fermée.
I wanted to buy bread, but The bakery was closed.
ou or
Tu veux du thé ou du café ?
Do you want tea? or Kaffee?
ou ... ou either ... or
C'est ou toi ou moi qui dois le faire.
Entweder du or I have to do it.
ou bien or (also)
On peut aller au cinéma, ou bien on reste ici.
We can go to the movies, or We'll stay here.
soit ... soit either ... or
Passez soit par la route nationale, soit par l'autoroute.
Drive entweder on the federal road or on the highway.

The following sentence serves as a reminder for the most common conjunctions: Mais où est donc Ornicar ? (mais, ou, et, donc, or, ni, car).
