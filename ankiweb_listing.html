Learn the 5000 most common words in French in context, with 149,711 example sentences, detailed grammar notes, audio and more. The ultimate deck for beginners and advanced (A1 to C1) who want to build a solid basic vocabulary and deepen their language skills. The 5000 most common words in a language make up up 95 percent of a written text and the 1000 most common words 85 percent of the spoken language.

Datengrundlagen

  • Deryle Lonsdale, Yvon Le Bras: A Frequency Dictionary of French (2009, Routledge). The underlying body comprises some 23 million French words, which come from a variety of written and spoken texts.
  • PONS Large Dictionary French–German, 2019
  • Le Robert Online Dictionary
  • Dictionnaires Larousse français
  • Dictionnaire de l’Académie française, 9e édition (2023)

Features

  • Audio: High quality machine-generated audio recordings for each word and example set.
  • IPA: International phonetic literacy for each word.
  • Definition: Precise and memorable translations, which have been handwritten over a period of more than two years. Keeped conspicuously short. No two definitions are the same.
  • Example sentences: On average, 30 sample sentences per word, a total of 149,711! Each sentence can be read by machine (Internet connection required), and the sentences are mixed during learning to convey the context optimally.
  • Konjugation: All conjugations of the 1185 included verbs in all important time forms, including marking irregular forms and options to hide and hide regular conjugations. To learn more about a time form, tap the respective line in the table.
  • Grammatik: An extensive library of 156 grammar entries at levels A1 to C1 helps to understand and apply French grammar. Each card can be accessed from the grammar library; it is included in the deck and also works offline.

Additional functions

  • Notiz: On 2568 maps additional information about grammar, meaning or use of the word is noted. Users can add additional notes or edit existing notes.
  • Wortarten: adj – Adjective, adv – Adverb, app – Apposition, art – Article, conj – Konjunktion, det – Determinativ, intj – Interjektion, n – Noun, num – Numeral, prep – Preposition, pro – Pronouns, v – Verb (i – intransitiv, t – transitiv, r – reflexiv, imp – Impersonal), f – feminin, i – Invariable, m – maskulinOn the map the meaning can be displayed by typing the word type.
  • Register: Some words are marked according to their frequency in specific contexts (e.g., "Sachtext" or "Mündlich").
  • Rank and dispersion: The first value is the rank of the word in the frequency list (what happens when you tap the rank?). The second value is the dispersion: a value of 27-100, which indicates how evenly a word occurs in different texts. The higher the value, the more even the distribution is.

Anleitung

  • Download the deck and import it to Anki.
  • The deck is divided into two sub-decks: French-German and German-French. This allows you to set individual options for each direction. For example, 15 new cards can be used every day in the direction of French-German, but only 10 new cards can be learned in the direction of French-German, because experience shows that the direction of French-German is easier to learn. If you want to learn only one direction, you can simply set the number of new cards to 0.

Lernmethodik

This is how you learn the most effectively with the deck:
  1. On the front of the card is a French word and an example sentence. Read the sentence and try to understand it.
  2. Then activate the gap text and assemble the translation.
  3. Once you're done, the French sentence will be read automatically. Repeat the sentence aloud to improve your pronunciation.
  4. Now you can tap the map to show the back. Here you will find the definition, the word type and pronunciation, and other example sentences.
  5. Evaluate how well you feel with the word and evaluate the card accordingly. If you feel safe, you can place it on a longer repetition interval.
The other direction (German-French) works the same way:
  1. On the front of the card is a German word and an example sentence. Read the sentence and try to express the meaning of the sentence in French. Also loud!
  2. Then activate the gap text and assemble the French sentence.
  3. Once you're done, the sentence will be read out automatically. Repeat the sentence loudly to improve your pronunciation.
  4. Now you can tap the card to display the back.

Tipps

  • Always learn the articles! Rate a card red if you are unsure about the article or gender of the noun.
  • Speak the example sentences aloud! This greatly improves the pronunciation and the feeling of speech.
  • Don't be discouraged by unknown words! These can usually be guessed from the context; try to grasp example sentences as a whole.
  • The definitions are deliberately kept short to allow an intuitive understanding of the words. Learning the definitions is less important than understanding the example sentences!
Even after learning the first 1000-1500 words, a solid basic vocabulary will be built up, which will significantly improve understanding and communication in French. With a learning workload of 30 cards or 15 new words per day, you can reach this base in two to three months. In order to further deepen and apply what you have learned, the podcast is InnerFrench highly recommended.

Personalisierung

On the back of the card you can set the following options (each for each direction):
  • hideSpoilers: By default, the translation of sentences is hidden and can be displayed by clicking. If the translation is to be displayed by default, set the value to false.
  • autoPlaySentence: Whether the first example sentence should be played automatically. autoPlaySentenceInGerman the sentence can be read in German.
  • tenses: Which conjugations should be displayed in the table. subjonctif imparfait is hidden by default, for example.
On the front of the card you can set the following options (each for each direction):
  • autoPlaySentence: Whether the example sentence should be read automatically.
  • autoPlaySentenceOnClozeFinish: Whether the example set should be read automatically when the gap game was finished.

FAQ

  • Why do I see only small rectangles instead of icons or symbols on some cards?

    This usually indicates that your Anki version is outdated. Please update Anki to the latest version.

  • The grammar library is not loaded — what can I do?

    In this case, it is usually helpful to update the deck: download the latest version and import it again to Anki. This will replace missing or outdated resources. Also check if the file has been downloaded completely and that no add-ons block the loading.
